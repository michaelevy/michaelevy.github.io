World Cup 2026 Team Strength

A composite rating of all 48 qualified teams, blending four sources. Click any column header to re-sort. Last refreshed 2026-05-28.

How it's calculated
  • 30% — Elo rating (eloratings.net)
  • 30% — Median outright winner odds across 9 bookmakers (DraftKings, Unibet, FanDuel, Betfair, BetRivers, William Hill, TAB…), via the Mundial '26 aggregate. Converted to implied probability.
  • 20% — Squad market value in €M (Transfermarkt, via Mundial '26)
  • 20% — Probability of reaching the final, per the Opta supercomputer (theanalyst.com)

Each input is min-max scaled to 0–100 across the 48 teams, then weighted and summed. Lower odds give higher scores.
